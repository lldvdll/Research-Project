## Decisions made this chat

**Stop chasing numeric reproduction of Song & Bogacz Fig 4d.** Reproduce the protocol; the claim under test is qualitative. This is a conceptual replication and is stated as such on the deviations slide.

**5×2 split for both scenarios.** Same partition, same schedule. Only the output layer differs — 10 units all active (Class-IL) or 2 shared units (Domain-IL). Scenario becomes the single variable. This is van de Ven's Split MNIST protocol, so baselines are comparable to a published table. It deviates from S&B (2 tasks × 5 classes, alternating), justified because their protocol varies task count and schedule alongside scenario.

**Class-IL primary, Domain-IL also run.** Class-IL because it has the largest dynamic range between methods, is the standard ML benchmark, matches the common biological case, and exercises both trunk and output layer so forgetting can be attributed. Domain-IL because it is the scenario the interference claim was demonstrated in, and it isolates trunk drift.

**The scenario-boundary idea is parked.** S&B Fig 4d used five shared output neurons, so it is Domain-IL — verified in their Methods. That's real, but it was briefly promoted to the talk's thesis without results to support it. Retired to `knowledge_base.md` §9.1; revisit after E4/E5/E6. No slide presupposes the answer.

**EqProp is a contrast, not a second competitor** — strong clamp (PC) vs weak clamp (EqProp). If PC separates from backprop and EqProp doesn't, the effect is full relaxation, not energy-based-ness generally.

**All experiments re-run.** No old plots.
